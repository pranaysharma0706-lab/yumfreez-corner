Our Story

YumFreez Corner began as the dream of a woman who wanted to build something of her own — a small local corner where her thoughts, her talent and her love for good food could reach the people around her.

What started as a single idea on 4th September 2026 has grown into a warm, welcoming spot where every snack is made with care and every customer is treated like family, with her husband standing by her side at every step, supporting her dream and helping it come to life.

YumFreez Corner serves fresh momos, pani puri, chaat, Nepal style chatpate, pakora, tea and coffee, along with everyday essentials like chips, biscuits, cold drinks and daily use items — all in one friendly place, made with the same care and love with which it all began.
